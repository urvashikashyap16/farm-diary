Farm Diary – prototype

Clickable demo for the SEVA First video. Software only; the real app runs offline speech on the phone.

Video flow (≈ 2 min of screen time)

  1. Switch the phone to No internet (top bar)
  2. Tap the mic, say or pick a phrase
  3. Check the entry, tap Yes, save
  4. Add a sale and a harvest the same way
  5. Open Summary, tap Read aloud
  6. Switch internet on: saved entries sync

Phrases to try (tap to speak them)

Demo notes: the phone here uses typed or tapped phrases and your browser's voice for read-aloud. Sample entries are illustrative, not field data. In the app, speech recognition (Vosk) and the parser run fully on the phone.
